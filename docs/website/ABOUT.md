# About Sumanth Gumedelli

Sumanth Gumedelli is a Senior SDET and QA Automation Architect focused on building reusable test platforms, automation frameworks, developer tooling, and **GenAI-powered quality engineering tools and governed Agentic AI workflows**.

Across more than 14 years in software engineering and quality engineering, his work has included web, API, mobile, database, performance, accessibility, cloud, and CI/CD automation. A recurring theme in his career has been framework and platform work: analyzing a testing problem, selecting an architecture, building reusable capabilities, integrating them into delivery pipelines, and enabling other engineers to adopt them.

SDETFlow is the public engineering expression of that approach: practical open-source libraries that work deterministically first, add GenAI and host-controlled Agentic AI where they create measurable value, and keep security, maintainability, and developer experience at the center.
